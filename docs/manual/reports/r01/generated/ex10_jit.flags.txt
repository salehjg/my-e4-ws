CompilerInfo.get_default()     -> GccInfo
GccInfo().cxx(), cxxflags()    -> 'g++', ['-DNDEBUG', '-std=c++17', '-fPIC', '-Ofast', '-fopenmp', '-march=native']
ClangInfo(...).cxx(), cxxflags() -> 'clang++', ['-DNDEBUG', '-std=c++17', '-fPIC', '-O3', '-fopenmp', '-march=native', '-Wall']
GccInfo(target=X86_AVX512)     -> -march=x86-64-v4
GccInfo(target=ARM_SVE)        -> -march=armv8.2-a+sve
